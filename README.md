# HR-Analytics Dashboard - Employee Attrition Analysis


## Project Overview

This project uses Microsoft Power BI to analyze employee attrition and workforce trends. The interactive dashboard helps explore employee turnover by job role, gender, education, age, salary, and years at the company.

## Objectives

Analyze employee attrition and attrition rate.

Identify job roles and employee groups with higher attrition.

Explore workforce demographics and salary distribution.

Present HR metrics through an interactive dashboard.

Support data-driven employee retention decisions.

## Key Features

KPI Cards: Display total employees, average age, attrition count, attrition rate, and average salary.

Job Role Analysis: Compare employee attrition across different job roles.

Demographic Analysis: Explore attrition by gender, age group, and education.

Salary Analysis: Examine attrition across salary ranges.

Tenure Analysis: Analyze attrition by years at the company.

Department Filters: Explore data for Human Resources, Research & Development, and Sales.

Key Insights

The dashboard displays 961 employees and 133 attrition cases.

The displayed attrition rate is approximately 14%.

The average employee age is 37 years.

Laboratory Technicians and Research Scientists account for notable attrition counts.

Male employees represent a larger share of the displayed attrition cases.

Attrition varies across education categories, salary ranges, and years at the company.
